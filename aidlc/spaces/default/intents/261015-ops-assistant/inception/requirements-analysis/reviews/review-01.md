### Summary

The requirements map well to the intent. All four success criteria and the four non-negotiable rules have a home, the Q&A answers are reflected faithfully (Q3, Q4, Q5, Q8, Q10, Q11), and most items have measurable thresholds. They are not ready for design: the read-only and redaction guarantees do not cover the AI session itself, the redaction tests cover only part of the stated redaction list, the Prometheus/Loki access rules are circular, and key bad-image-tag edge cases (auth-versus-missing-tag, a stale local clone) are unspecified.
