### Summary

The decomposition is sound: one reader per source, a mandatory output pipeline, a separate Redactor and a fail-closed guard. The catalogue is acyclic, and the human view matches the YAML. The security guarantees are asserted rather than made structural in five places: the verified identity is not bound to the readers, the single-exit rule has bypass paths, the git runner is a deny-list, the redaction and protected-identifier precedence is undefined, and the fixture replay design is incomplete. These should be settled before Functional Design.
